# HomeValue rubric — 100 points

## Predictive performance — 40 points

- 10: valid submission that beats the median baseline.
- 15: public leaderboard performance, normalized between the baseline and reference benchmark.
- 15: private leaderboard performance, normalized the same way.

## Methodology — 35 points

- 8: correct problem framing and unit of analysis.
- 8: defensible validation design with no preprocessing leakage.
- 8: appropriate missing-value and categorical-feature handling.
- 6: meaningful baseline and model comparison.
- 5: residual and segment-level error analysis.

## Business communication — 25 points

- 8: interprets coefficients carefully without claiming causality.
- 7: translates errors into acquisition risk.
- 6: provides actionable guardrails and limitations.
- 4: clear, concise, executive-ready memo and complete AI disclosure.

Any use of prohibited external labels or unreproducible manual predictions results in a methodology score of zero and referral under the academic-integrity policy.
