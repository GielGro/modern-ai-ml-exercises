# Segment Studio rubric — 100 points

## Analytical validity — 40

- 10: correct unit of analysis, transformations, and scale treatment.
- 10: comparison across `k`, algorithms, and stability checks.
- 8: thoughtful outlier handling without silently discarding valuable customers.
- 7: correct PCA interpretation and responsible use of UMAP.
- 5: reproducible assignments with no missing customers.

## Segment usefulness — 35

- 10: distinct and quantitatively supported profiles.
- 10: actionable differentiated strategies.
- 8: segment names avoid unsupported demographic or psychological claims.
- 7: limitations and operational refresh plan.

## Communication — 25

- 10: executive-quality memo.
- 8: effective visualization and presentation.
- 4: constructive peer critique.
- 3: complete AI-use disclosure.
