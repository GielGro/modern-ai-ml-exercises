# Causal Campaign rubric — 100 points

## Causal design and estimation — 45

- 10: precise estimand, DAG, assumptions, and adjustment set.
- 10: overlap and covariate-balance diagnostics.
- 12: correct weighting/matching and doubly robust or outcome-regression estimate.
- 7: valid uncertainty quantification.
- 6: meaningful sensitivity or refutation analysis.

## Evidence and decision — 30

- 10: effect magnitudes interpreted in business units.
- 8: differences between naive and adjusted estimates explained.
- 7: recommendation respects uncertainty and identification limits.
- 5: thoughtful next-experiment design.

## Responsible AI and communication — 25

- 8: fairness and targeting audit.
- 6: SHAP, if used, is framed correctly and not causally overclaimed.
- 7: executive-quality memo and visuals.
- 4: reproducibility and AI-use disclosure.
