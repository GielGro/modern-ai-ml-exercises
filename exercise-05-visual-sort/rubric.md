# VisualSort rubric — 100 points

## Model performance — 35

- 10: credible baseline and valid submission.
- 15: private macro-F1 performance.
- 10: class-level recall and confidence-review performance.

## Methodology — 40

- 8: correct image audit and validation design.
- 10: MLP training, learning curves, and overfitting controls.
- 12: technically sound transfer-learning and fine-tuning workflow.
- 5: systematic error analysis.
- 5: runtime, model-size, and reproducibility discipline.

## Communication and responsible use — 25

- 8: deployable human-review policy.
- 7: specific failure modes and limitations.
- 6: clear demo and model card.
- 4: complete AI-use disclosure.
