# Evaluation principles

Predictive performance is evidence, not the whole grade. Strong work demonstrates a valid experimental design, clear reasoning, reproducibility, and a decision-relevant interpretation.

Students should rely on their documented validation evidence while developing a model. After the deadline, the instructor may evaluate submitted outputs against withheld outcomes. A large validation-to-withheld deterioration is evidence of overfitting or distribution shift and should be discussed, not hidden.

Students may discuss concepts, but every submitted notebook, experiment record, and memo must be their own work.
