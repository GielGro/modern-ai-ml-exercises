# Evaluation principles

Leaderboard position is evidence, not the whole grade. Strong work demonstrates a valid experimental design, clear reasoning, reproducibility, and a decision-relevant interpretation.

Public leaderboard results are provisional. Final performance is computed on the private test partition after the deadline. Large public-to-private deterioration should be discussed as evidence of overfitting.

Students may discuss concepts, but every submitted notebook, experiment record, and memo must be their own work.
